DOCUMENT ID: doc_03
TYPE: Forensic Autopsy Report
SOURCE: District Forensic Laboratory

SUBJECT: Aditya Rao
CAUSE OF DEATH: Acute potassium cyanide poisoning.

Toxicology confirms cyanide was present in the whiskey glass recovered from
the scene (doc_02) at a lethal concentration. Stomach contents indicate
ingestion occurred approximately 20-35 minutes before death, placing the
poisoning window between 11:12 PM and 11:27 PM.

Cyanide of this purity is not commonly available; the lab notes it is
consistent with compounds used in certain industrial/laboratory settings,
not household products. This substantially narrows the pool of people with
plausible access to the poison.

No other guests at the retreat exhibited any symptoms consistent with
poisoning, indicating the substance was introduced directly and specifically
into the victim's glass rather than a shared source (e.g., a punch bowl).

Time of death: 11:44 PM (± 3 minutes), consistent with witness reports of
the victim collapsing at approximately 11:47 PM.
