TYPE: Initial Incident Report
SOURCE: Local Police Department, Case #SK-2291

INCIDENT: Sudden death, Aditya Rao (34)
LOCATION: Lakeview Resort, outdoor bar area, Pavilion 2
TIME REPORTED: 11:52 PM

Responding officers found the victim collapsed near the outdoor bar counter.
Bartender on duty (contracted caterer, not a Skyline employee) had already
left the immediate area by the time officers arrived, per witness accounts.
Approximately 40 guests were present at the retreat party, with an estimated
14 people in the immediate vicinity of the bar between 11:00 PM and 11:45 PM.

A half-finished glass of whiskey believed to belong to the victim was
recovered from the bar counter and sent for toxicology. No other guests
reported symptoms of poisoning.

Four individuals have been identified by multiple witnesses as having been
near the bar area alone with the victim's glass unattended at some point
during the relevant window:
- Priya Nair (Co-Founder & CTO)
- Vikram Shah (Lead Investor, Board Member)
- Meera Iyer (Head of HR)
- Rohan Desai (Junior Financial Analyst)

Case has been escalated to detective division pending toxicology results.
