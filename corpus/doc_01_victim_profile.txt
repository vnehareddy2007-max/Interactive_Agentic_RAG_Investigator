SOURCE: HR Personnel File

VICTIM: Aditya Rao, 34
ROLE: Co-Founder & CEO, Skyline Analytics Pvt Ltd
JOINED: Company founded 6 years ago with Priya Nair.

Aditya Rao built Skyline Analytics from a two-person garage startup into a
120-employee data analytics firm. Known for being demanding but fair, he
had recently been under pressure from the board (led by investor Vikram Shah)
to pivot the company's product line ahead of a Series C funding round.

Two weeks before his death, Aditya scheduled an internal financial audit,
citing "irregularities noticed in Q3 vendor payments." The audit was set to
begin the Monday after the company retreat.

Aditya was unmarried. Colleagues describe him as intensely private about
his personal life. HR records show a formal complaint was filed against him
eight months ago by Meera Iyer (Head of HR) for "conduct concerns," which
was later withdrawn.

The retreat was held at a lakeside resort to celebrate the company's Series B
anniversary. Aditya was found unresponsive at 11:47 PM near the outdoor bar
area during the closing night party.
