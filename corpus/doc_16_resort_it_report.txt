RE: CCTV Camera 3 (Pavilion 2 Bar Area) signal interruption, 11:12-11:18 PM

Our review of the network switch logs shows the camera lost connection due
to a physical disconnection at the junction box located behind the bar
counter, not a software or power failure. The junction box is accessible
only by ducking behind the bar counter itself, an area guests do not
normally have reason to access.

We have no camera footage of who caused the disconnection, as the outage
by definition affected the only camera covering that specific angle.
Backup camera 4, covering the pavilion entrance from a distance, shows
several guests passing in the background during this window but is too far
to identify actions at the bar counter itself.

This incident has been flagged as suspicious and shared with investigators.
